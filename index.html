Our Laundry Services

laundry services are maintained in the most modern ways like door pickup and delivery with multiple types of dry cleaning facilities along with the regular wash.

Washing

This is the process of cleaning clothes using water and detergent. Laundry services may use commercial-grade washing machines that can handle large loads of laundry efficiently.

Drying

After washing, clothes need to be dried. This can be done using tumble dryers or hanging them to air dry, depending on the preferences of the laundry service and the type of fabric being dried.

Ironing/Pressing

Certain items may require ironing or pressing to remove wrinkles and creases. This step is often included in premium laundry services or can be requested as an add-on service. & they are typically folded neatly.

Pickup and Delivery

Many laundry services offer pickup and delivery options for added convenience. Customers can schedule a pickup, and their clean clothes will be delivered back to them once the laundry is done.
